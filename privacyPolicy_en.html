2. General Information About Data Processing

Protecting your personal data is important to me. I process personal data only in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

The “Household – Budget Tracker” app allows you to manage personal financial information such as income, expenses, accounts, budgets, and financial goals digitally.

In particular:

  • There is no connection to banks or other financial institutions.
  • No bank accounts are automatically accessed or read.
  • No online banking credentials are processed.
  • No IBANs or credit card numbers are required or stored.
  • Financial data is generally entered into the app by you.
  • No advertising or tracking services are used.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services are used.

3. What Personal Data Is Processed?

Depending on how you use the app, the following data may be processed.

3.1 User Account and Authentication

When you use a user account, the data required for authentication are processed.

Depending on the sign-in method you choose, this may include:

  • an email address when registering with email and password,
  • authentication information provided by Google as part of Google Sign-In,
  • a technical user identifier,
  • authentication and session information.

The email address is not stored in the users table used by the app. However, it may be processed by the authentication service as part of the authentication process.

When using guest login, providing an email address or a password is not required.

3.2 Household and Financial Data Entered by You

The app allows you to store data that you enter yourself or create within the app. This may include, in particular:

  • transactions, including income, expenses, and transfers,
  • transaction descriptions and purposes,
  • amounts,
  • transaction dates,
  • accounts and account names,
  • budgets,
  • categories,
  • financial goals,
  • completed financial goals,
  • information about people you voluntarily enter in a transaction,
  • other content you enter within the app.

Because you may also enter the names of other people in transactions, you should only enter personal data of other people whose processing is necessary for your personal purpose.

3.3 Settings

To provide and personalize the app, the following settings may also be stored:

  • preferred language,
  • currency,
  • time zone,
  • dashboard settings,
  • other personal app settings.

3.4 Subscriptions and RevenueCat

For the purchase and management of paid subscriptions, the app uses the RevenueCat service.

In connection with a subscription, RevenueCat processes information about purchases and transactions (such as purchase or subscription status) and a technical user identifier, where required to associate the subscription with the user. RevenueCat may also receive technical information about the device used and app usage where necessary to provide the service.

The actual payment is processed through the respective app store, in particular Google Play or the Apple App Store. RevenueCat is used primarily for the technical management, validation, and provision of subscription status within the app.

According to its own statements, RevenueCat processes end-user personal data as a processor on behalf of the respective app provider. More information is available in the RevenueCat Privacy Policy .

3.5 Feedback and Bug Reports

When you use the “Submit Feedback” or “Report a Bug” features to send me feedback, the data you provide in the process are processed.

This includes, in particular, your email address, the feedback or bug report you enter, and any other content you voluntarily provide.

The submitted data is transmitted to GitHub and stored there for processing your feedback, communicating with you, investigating reported bugs, and improving the app. GitHub is used as a technical service provider in this process.

Please do not submit unnecessary personal data, especially sensitive data or confidential financial information, through these features.

GitHub stores and processes personal data, among other places, in the United States and other countries. For transfers from the European Union, GitHub states that it uses Standard Contractual Clauses and participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. More information is available in the GitHub Privacy Statement .

  1. Providing the app and its features
    So that you can save and manage your transactions, accounts, budgets, categories, and financial goals.
  2. Managing your user account

  3. Synchronizing your data
    To ensure that the data stored within the app is available on your devices and accessible on new or multiple devices.

    For storing and using your language, currency, time zone, and dashboard settings.
  5. Performing requested functions

  7. Managing paid subscriptions
    For the verification, management, and provision of the premium features you have purchased.
  8. Processing feedback and bug reports
    To process your feedback, communicate with you, investigate reported errors, and improve the app.
  9. Compliance with legal obligations
    Where required by law.

Processing is carried out in accordance with the principles of purpose limitation, data minimization, and storage limitation.

5. Legal Bases for Processing

Depending on the processing activity, personal data is processed on the legal bases described by Article 6(1) GDPR.

Article 6(1)(b) GDPR – Contract Performance

Where processing is necessary to provide the features you request and your use of the app, it is carried out for the performance of the user relationship.

This includes, in particular:

  • user account management,
  • storage of your transactions,
  • storage of your accounts,
  • storage of your budgets,
  • storage of your categories,
  • storage of your financial goals,
  • storage of your completed financial goals,
  • storage of your personal settings.

Article 6(1)(c) GDPR – Legal Obligation

Where personal data must be processed due to legal obligations, must be processed, the processing takes place on the basis of Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

Article 6(1)(b) GDPR – Subscriptions

Processing of data necessary for entering into, managing, and providing a paid subscription you request is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Article 6(1)(f) GDPR – Legitimate Interests

Where necessary, processing may be based on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, in particular to ensure technical and information technology security and the prevention of misuse.

9. No Bank Connectivity

The app has no interface with banks or other financial institutions.

In particular:

  • bank balances are not retrieved from banks,
  • bank transactions are not automatically imported,
  • online banking credentials are not stored,
  • IBANs are not required or stored,
  • No credit card details are required or stored.

Financial data displayed in the app generally comes from user-entered information.

12. Data Retention

As a general rule, personal data is stored only for as long as is necessary for the respective purposes.

Data you store in the app generally remains stored for as long as your user account exists and you do not delete the data yourself.

You can delete your user account and associated data using the account deletion function provided in the app.

When your user account is deleted, the app data associated with your account is deleted unless legal retention requirements or other legally permissible reasons prevent immediate deletion.

Feedback and bug reports submitted through GitHub are generally retained for as long as necessary for processing, follow-up, and documentation of the relevant matter.

Subscription information may be retained by RevenueCat in accordance with its applicable retention periods where necessary to manage the subscription, comply with legal obligations, or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Legal retention requirements remain unaffected.

13. Account Deletion

You can delete your user account at any time using the designated function within the app.

Deletion generally also removes the data associated with your user account, including in particular:

  • transactions,
  • accounts,
  • budgets,
  • categories,
  • financial goals,
  • completed financial goals,
  • dashboard settings,
  • other stored app data.

Deletion does not necessarily apply to data that must continue to be retained due to legal obligations.

Data you submitted through the “Submit Feedback” or “Report a Bug” features to GitHub are not automatically removed from GitHub solely by deleting your app user account. A separate deletion request may be required for such data. You may contact the contact address above for this purpose.

RevenueCat may also retain data relating to completed or previous subscriptions due to its applicable retention periods or legal obligations beyond deletion of the app account.
